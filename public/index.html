Base allocation (25 Sq.Ft)

Edit a base qty and the projected stock, statuses, KPIs and effective space update everywhere at once. Material names can't change — a new material has no column in the Report tab, so there is nothing to count it from.

Sq.Ft multipliers

A band's multiplier decides the projected stock for stores of that size. Add a band above to cover a new size (for example 300 Sq.Ft).

S2W limits

Maximum S2W stock allowed for each store size. Stores above the limit show as Limit Breached.

Change history

Rule changes made in this browser, newest first. The site has no login, so history is kept on this device only.

Matched tolerance: ±1%

A material or a store whose variance is within ±1% of its projected quantity counts as Matched (e.g. Cat 6 at −0.5% variance is Matched, not Shortage). Outside the tolerance, the sign of the variance decides Excess / Shortage.
